The Quantum Information research area brings together studies on theoretical and conceptual aspects of quantum physics, with an emphasis on problems involving information, foundations, quantum systems, and their different forms of description.

The research developed in this area addresses different questions in quantum theory and explores mathematical and computational tools to investigate relevant properties, processes, and phenomena.

This description is provisional and will be complemented with contributions from the group's researchers.
