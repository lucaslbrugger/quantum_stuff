The Applied Physics research area brings together research exploring physical concepts, methods, and tools in the investigation of different systems and problems.

The work developed in this area may involve theoretical, experimental, and computational aspects, seeking to understand physical phenomena and their possible applications in different contexts.

This description is provisional and will be complemented with contributions from the group's researchers.
