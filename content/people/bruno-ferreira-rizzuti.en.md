I hold Bachelor's degrees in Physics (2006) and Mathematics (2008), a Master's degree (2008), and a PhD in Physics (2012), all from the Federal University of Juiz de Fora. I work in quantum theory, mathematical physics, and science education. My research includes: (i) foundations of quantum mechanics and quantum theory from a Bayesian perspective; (ii) the analysis of constrained systems with local symmetries and quantization; (iii) applications of operational approaches to the description of physical systems; and (iv) prototyping visually attractive products for physics education, with classroom applications through inquiry-based teaching. I am currently an Associate Professor 4 in the Department of Physics at the Federal University of Juiz de Fora.
