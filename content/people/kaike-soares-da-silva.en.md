Kaike Soares da Silva holds a technical degree in Chemistry from the Federal Center for Technological Education Celso Suckow da Fonseca (CEFET-RJ, Valença Campus) and is currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Physics at the Federal University of Juiz de Fora (UFJF).

From 2024 to 2025, his research was centered on computational materials science and condensed matter physics, specifically focusing on hydrogen storage mechanisms. He utilized Density Functional Theory (DFT) and extended Tight-Binding (xTB) methods for molecular dynamics simulations, investigating graphene and graphyne doping to optimize hydrogen storage efficiency.

Currently supervised by Prof. Dr. Bruno Rizzuti, Kaike is studying the mathematical foundations underlying quantum mechanics and quantum information theory, with plans to directly investigate open problems in quantum information and quantum foundations in the near future.
