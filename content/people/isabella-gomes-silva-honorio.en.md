An undergraduate research student exploring the intersection of Quantum Information Theory and Quantum Computation. My current work focuses on quantum Hamiltonian simulation, specifically applying and optimizing novel Rodeo Algorithms to efficiently extract energy spectra and prepare target eigenstates on quantum hardware. By leveraging these probabilistic filtering techniques, my research aims to tackle complex many-body Hamiltonians and push the boundaries of NISQ-era applications in physics and chemistry.
