I am currently a PhD student in Physics at PPGF-UFJF, working in quantum information and the foundations of quantum mechanics. I completed my Bachelor's degree at UFJF in 2023, with a thesis entitled “A modern perspective on the Stern-Gerlach experiment”. I entered the Master's program in Physics at PPGF-UFJF in 2023, where I investigated the emergence of dynamics in coarse-graining scenarios using generalized Bayesian inference techniques. I defended my Master's dissertation, entitled “Effective Descriptions in Quantum Mechanics: A Bayesian Inference Problem”, in 2025. Later in 2025, I entered the PhD program at the same institution, continuing my research on the foundations of quantum mechanics.
