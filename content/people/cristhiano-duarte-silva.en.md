I am a researcher whose work focuses on: Quantum Information Theory, Foundations of Probability and Foundations of Physics. I have been granted a fellowship from CNPq within the scope of the Conhecimento Brasil Program. At the moment I work as Invited Professor with the Federal University of Juiz de Fora, a Research Fellow at the Federal Univesity of Bahia and hold a position at the Maurício Grabois Foundation's Curator Board.  I was a visiting researcher, fully funded by the FQXi at the International Institute of Physics (Natal - Brazil). I was also a research fellow with the School for Physics and Astronomy at the University of Leeds. Before settling in the UK, I was a visiting researcher at the prestigious Wigner Centre for Physics (Budapest - Hungary). At Chapman University I held a postdoctoral  position mixing teaching and research in a program called Grand Challenges Initiative. Before that I was a postdoc at the International Institute of Physics (Natal - Brazil). I was also a postdoc at two other institutions: right after my PhD I got a position at the Federal University of Minas Gerais (Brazil), following that I got a (brief summer postdoctoral) position at the Brazilian Institute for Pure and Applied Mathematics (Rio de Janeiro - Brazil).
