I hold a Master's degree (2026), a Bachelor's degree in Physics (2025), and a Bachelor's degree in Exact Sciences (2023), all from the Federal University of Juiz de Fora (UFJF). My main interests are the foundations of quantum mechanics, quantum information, and mathematical physics. I am currently working on quantum foundations, exploring quantum mechanics as a noncommutative generalization of Bayesian inference. I seek to advance this formalism by applying it to scenarios of interest, such as Wigner's friend paradox, the inferential notion of quantum reference frames, and concepts of Bayesian compatibility and distinguishability.
