I am currently a PhD student in Applied Mathematics at IME-USP, working in the area of nonlinear optimization. I hold a Master's degree in Physics from UFJF, where my dissertation addressed a Quantum Information problem using computational methods based on semidefinite programming and numerical analysis. I also hold Bachelor's degrees in Physics and Exact Sciences from UFJF. My undergraduate theses covered topics ranging from the connection between Lie groups and Killing vector fields to the interpretability of attention-based AI models for the Knowledge Tracing problem. I am interested in applications of optimization and semidefinite programming in Quantum Information, as well as quantum machine learning methods.
