A research student exploring the intersection of Quantum Information Theory and Quantum Computation. Their current work focuses on quantum Hamiltonian simulation, specifically applying and optimizing novel Rodeo Algorithms to efficiently extract energy spectra and prepare target eigenstates on quantum hardware. By leveraging these probabilistic filtering techniques, their research aims to tackle complex many-body Hamiltonians and push the boundaries of NISQ-era for physics and chemistry applications.
