This paper revisits the Stern-Gerlach experiment using the contemporary language of prepare-and-measure scenarios. The proposal connects geometric and algebraic aspects of the state space with physical space and discusses simulations of the experiment as well as experimentally relevant statistical properties. The work also presents this framework as a possible entry point to quantum information theory and the foundations of quantum mechanics.
