This work investigates the emergence of effective quantum dynamics from coarse-grained descriptions, interpreting the problem from the perspective of quantum Bayesian inference. The analysis uses the quantum conditional states formalism and studies conditions for the existence of an emergent dynamics. When a global description cannot be obtained, the authors examine a solution based on the Petz recovery map and use semidefinite programming to investigate two-qubit scenarios. The work also introduces a robustness measure associated with compatibility between microscopic dynamics and a coarse-grained description.
