This paper investigates the operational connection between the preparation of two-level quantum systems and their description as states in a Hilbert space. To bridge the laboratory and the space of states, the authors use the Hopf fibration and present a simple optical realization. The construction also illustrates the need for two charts to cover a sphere and connects the experimental realization with the structure of the sphere as a smooth manifold.
